01 · Prepaid meter supply

Supply and commission STS-compliant prepaid meters at scale.

We source, import and commission single-phase and three-phase STS prepayment meters for distribution companies, metering contractors and estate developers across Nigeria.

02 · Mini-grid and off-grid metering

Revenue-ready metering for off-grid and mini-grid operators.

Mini-grid operators need to recover revenue reliably from customers with no connection to the national grid. That takes more than a meter. It takes a working vending service, a monitoring system and an engineer who can reach the site.

Live at the Ikere-Ekiti Mini-Grid, full stack from meters to token vending

03 · HES remote monitoring and management

Real-time visibility across every meter in your network.

Arotob operates a cloud-based Head End System (HES) that gives you live data and control across your deployed meters, without building or maintaining the infrastructure yourself.

Pricing is based on connected meter count and service level, by quotation.

Learn more →

What it provides

  • Real-time meter status and consumption data
  • Remote connect and disconnect
  • Tamper detection and fraud alerts
  • Token vending and credit management
  • Meter diagnostics

Offered as a managed service

This suits distribution companies that want the data without the IT overhead, mini-grid operators monitoring remote sites, and estate developers handing residents a working system.

04 · API and platform integration

Connect your customer platform directly to metering infrastructure.

If your business runs its own customer management, billing or payment platform, Arotob can connect it to metering infrastructure through an API, so token generation and meter data flow automatically, with no manual work.

This is the right model for payment operators, energy platform companies and DisCos that want their customer systems to talk directly to meters in the field.

Talk to our technical team →

How it works

  • Your platform triggers a token request and we handle the metering side
  • Real-time consumption and status data feeds into your dashboards
  • Works with existing payment platforms, including cross-border payment services
